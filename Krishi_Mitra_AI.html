How it works

Local JavaScript workflow

1 · ReceiveYour message is sent to the local Node.js service.
2 · RetrieveKeyword-based document retrieval finds matching passages you uploaded.
3 · ToolsLocal endpoints register GPS, photo metadata and review requests.
4 · ExplainThe site shows source passages and warns when no source is available.
Capability note: This version has no language model, no external AI service and no official PMFBY API. It is a local retrieval and workflow prototype, not unrestricted conversational AI.

Responsible AI

The system must not invent eligibility, deadlines, claim status, weather evidence or approval. Government/insurer authorities make official decisions.

Krishi Mitra AI is an independent educational prototype, not an official Government of India, PMFBY or insurer website. It does not file, assess or approve claims. Confirm all guidance with the current applicable official notification and authorized insurer.
